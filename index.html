About Us

Located in Calgary, Alberta, Vista Geomatics is a multi-disciplinary firm that provides a wide array of surveying services. Our team is composed of experienced professionals, including Alberta Land Surveyors (ALS), Project Managers, Certified Survey Technologists, Geomatics Engineering Technologists, and CAD technicians.

At Vista Geomatics, we aim to lead the land surveying industry by offering outstanding products and unmatched value and service to our clients. We accomplish this through personalized attention, a skilled workforce, and a commitment to precise data collection and reporting.

Services

At Vista Geomatics, we offer a wide range of surveying services tailored to meet the needs of various industries. Our expertise includes municipal surveys, oil and gas surveys, and construction surveys.

Municipal Surveys: Real Property Reports, subdivision plans, condominium plans.

Oil and Gas Surveys: Precise data and analysis for exploration and production activities.

Construction Surveys: Support across all phases of construction, accuracy and efficiency from start to finish.

Our professional land surveyors are dedicated to delivering exceptional service and quality results for every project.
