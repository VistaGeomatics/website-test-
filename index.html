Company Overview

Professional expertise.
Personalized service.

Vista Geomatics is a Calgary-based multidisciplinary land surveying company serving clients throughout Alberta. Our team includes Alberta Land Surveyors, project managers, survey and geomatics technologists, field personnel, and CAD technicians.

We combine technical experience, current survey technology, and responsive project support to deliver accurate data and dependable results across municipal, construction, oil and gas, and aerial survey projects.

Capabilities

Survey services for every
stage of the project.

From initial site data to final as-built verification, our crews provide field-to-finish support tailored to the work.

Municipal & Legal Surveys

Real Property Reports, property line surveys, development permits, subdivisions, grading certificates, condominium surveys, and new-home surveys.

Construction Layout

Deep and shallow utilities, roads, bridges, earthworks, gridlines, piles, anchor bolts, structural layout, and surface improvements.

Oil & Gas Surveying

Well-site and pipeline surveys, underground facility locations, pre-engineering surveys, cadastral work, and land-acquisition support.

Drone & UAV Surveying

High-resolution aerial mapping, orthomosaics, topographic data, site documentation, progress imagery, stockpile volumes, and large-area capture.

Topographic Surveys

Detailed surface, feature, and elevation data for planning, engineering, design, development, drainage, and construction.

As-Built & Monitoring

As-built verification, volume reporting, deformation monitoring, underground locate surveys, and data preparation for machine-control systems.
